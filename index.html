Current research

African Nuclear Standards and Technology Development Initiative

Richards Okiemute, Fellow

ASME Engineering for Change (E4C) Fellowship, in partnership with the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME). Ongoing.

A structured assessment of the nuclear energy landscape across African nations: mapping national energy plans and key stakeholders, documenting knowledge gaps in nuclear technology standards, and reviewing the investment landscape. The output is a region-specific intelligence package to guide standards development and Africa–EU collaboration.
